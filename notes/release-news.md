# What's new, per release

tools/release.py puts the section of the core version being packaged into the release text ("What's new"). One
short, plain line per change, written for players; write the section before `release.py package`.

## v1.6.0
- **Own icons**: Dalamadur, Shah Dalamadur and Tigerstripe Zamtrios now show their own MH4U icons in menus, on the
  target camera and on the quest board (they borrowed Kushala Daora's and Zamtrios' icons). Dalamadur, Shah
  Dalamadur and Gogmazios quests show MH4U's "?" on the board, as in MH4U.
- **Size records**: the Hunter's Notes size records of the new monsters show the right monster and real sizes
  instead of rocks.
- **Dalamadur**: no more lake damage while you stand on his back. Running off his back plays the jump again, so you
  can reach the walls. His move to another spot no longer restarts its countdown each time he flinches or topples.
- **Tigerstripe Zamtrios**: now a G★3 quest (it was G★1, below MHGU's own Zamtrios) with matching health, damage and
  rewards. His water beam now inflicts water instead of ice.
